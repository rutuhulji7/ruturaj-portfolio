How I work

What "Product Owner" actually means on my desk day to day
01

Backlog & sprint ownership

I prioritize and groom the backlog and plan sprints myself — currently running this across 100+ live user stories without letting scope drift or delivery predictability slip.

02

Stakeholder alignment

I sit between engineering, design, and business stakeholders across multiple enterprise accounts at once, translating priorities both ways so releases don't stall on miscommunication.

03

Every feature starts as a documented BRD or FRD with clear acceptance criteria — the discipline behind 50+ requirement documents and a consistently high stakeholder sign-off rate.
